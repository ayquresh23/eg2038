# EG2038 — Microstructure Evolution & Control in Metallic Materials

Study site for EG2038 (Microstructure Evolution & Control in Metallic Materials: Theory & Practicals) at Swansea University.

**Live site:** https://ayquresh23.github.io/eg2038/

## Contents

- Unit 1: Introduction to Thermodynamics (why microstructure matters, thermodynamics vs kinetics, stability & metastability, terminology, first/second law, enthalpy, entropy, Gibbs free energy, measuring the parameters)
- Unit 2: Solution Thermodynamics (free energy of mixing, ideal and regular solutions, ordering and clustering, real solutions and intermetallics, chemical potential)
- Unit 3: Using Phase Diagrams (phase identification and tie lines, the lever rule, mass vs volume fraction, microstructure development in isomorphous, eutectic and peritectic systems, why eutectics are lamellar)
- Unit 4: Construction of Phase Diagrams (free energy curves and the common tangent, building an isomorphous diagram, miscibility gaps, ordered alloys, the four invariant reactions, eutectic and peritectic diagram types, congruent transformations)
- Unit 5: Limitations of Thermodynamics (coring, activation energy and the Arrhenius law, Fick's first and second laws, vacancy vs interstitial diffusion, chemical potential as the driver of diffusion)
- Unit 6: Solidification: Nucleation (driving force, cooling curves, homogeneous nucleation, critical radius and energy barrier, heterogeneous nucleation and the spherical cap, inoculation and Hall-Petch; interactive nucleation barrier explorer)
- Unit 7: Solidification: Growth (interface stability, dendrites, solute rejection, constitutional supercooling and the four growth forms, faceted vs non-faceted growth, the ingot structure, chemical/low-superheat/physical grain refinement, turbine blade and metallic glass case studies; interactive constitutional supercooling explorer)
- 155 glossary terms with flashcard mode
- Formula sheet (37 blocks)
- 75 MCQ trap cards
- Units 8-10 (Metalforming, Fe-C System, Precipitation Strengthening) will be added as they're covered in lectures
